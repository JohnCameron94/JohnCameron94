## 💼 Services Provided

### ☁️ Cloud Workspace Migration & Setup

Helping businesses transition from legacy systems to modern cloud-based productivity platforms:

- **Microsoft 365 tenant setup** — Domain configuration, licensing, user provisioning, and security baseline implementation
- **Google Workspace deployment** — Account setup, organizational units, shared drives, and policy configurations
- **Email migration** — Seamless transition from on-premises Exchange, POP/IMAP providers, or competing platforms with minimal downtime
- **DNS & domain configuration** — SPF, DKIM, DMARC records to ensure email deliverability and security
- **SSO & MFA implementation** — Enforcing modern authentication standards and conditional access policies

### 📧 Email & Communication Systems

Managing cloud-based email infrastructure:

- **Microsoft 365 Exchange Online** configuration and administration
- **Google Workspace Gmail** setup and management
- **Email security hardening** — Anti-spam, anti-phishing, and TLS transport encryption
- **Email routing & forwarding** configuration
- **Backup & disaster recovery** strategies for critical business communications

### 🌐 Website Management & Hosting

Supporting businesses with web presence and hosting infrastructure:

- **Wix website management** — Site setup, customization, and ongoing maintenance
- **WordPress administration** — Installation, configuration, theme/plugin management, security hardening
- **DNS hosting management** — Domain registration, DNS configuration, and record management
- **Website migrations** — Seamless transitions between hosting providers
- **SSL certificate setup** for secure HTTPS connections
- **Performance optimization** — Caching, CDN configuration, and load time improvements

### 🔒 Device Management & Security (Microsoft Intune)

Implementing modern endpoint management for distributed workforces:

- **Microsoft Intune enrollment** for Windows, macOS, iOS, and Android devices
- **Configuration profiles** enforcing security baselines, compliance policies, and device restrictions
- **Conditional access policies** ensuring only compliant, managed devices can access corporate resources
- **App deployment & management** via Intune to streamline software distribution
- **Mobile Device Management (MDM)** for BYOD scenarios with privacy-first configurations
- **Windows Autopilot** setup for zero-touch device provisioning

### 🌐 Network Infrastructure Setup

Designing and implementing secure, reliable business networks:

- **Wireless access point installation** — Strategic placement for optimal coverage and performance
- **Router configuration** — Firewall rules, port forwarding, VPN setup, and QoS management
- **Dual-band WiFi optimization** — 2.4GHz and 5GHz band configuration for device compatibility and performance
- **Network segmentation** — Separate public guest networks from private business networks
- **VLAN configuration** for enhanced security and traffic isolation
- **Network security hardening** — WPA3 encryption, MAC filtering, and intrusion prevention
- **IP addressing schemes** — DHCP configuration and static IP assignment
- **Network troubleshooting** — Diagnosing connectivity issues, bandwidth optimization, and performance monitoring

### 🛠️ General IT Infrastructure Consulting

- **Azure Active Directory (AAD)** user and group management
- **Shared resource configuration** — SharePoint, OneDrive, Teams site architecture
- **License optimization** to reduce costs while maintaining functionality
- **Compliance & governance** alignment (PIPEDA, GDPR where applicable)
- **Training & documentation** for internal IT staff and end users
