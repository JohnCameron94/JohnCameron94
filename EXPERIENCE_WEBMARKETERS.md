- 🔐 Maintain strict HIPAA-compliant data handling

## 🌡️ What It's Like Coding for Healthcare

Healthcare software isn't like shipping a SaaS landing page. Every UI decision matters:

- **Clarity > Cleverness** — A surgeon reading vitals on a 6-inch screen needs information immediately. No clutter, no ambiguity.
- **Reliability is non-negotiable** — Edge cases that might cause a minor annoyance in a social app can mean real consequences here.
- **Compliance baked in** — HIPAA requirements shape everything from data display to session management.
- **Performance on real devices** — Clinical tablets and locked-down enterprise phones aren't MacBook Pros. Optimization is a first-class concern.

It's a genuinely humbling and exciting space to work in. 🩺
